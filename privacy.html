Stardust Tales — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Note on this document: this policy accurately describes how the current version of the app handles data, based on its actual source code. It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. Because Stardust Tales is used by parents on behalf of children, we recommend a legal review before relying on this policy at scale — see the "Contact" section to report any concern.

What information we collect

When you use Stardust Tales, the parent or guardian provides: a child's first name, age (or age range), a favorite color, and a preferred story theme. Optionally, a photo of the child may be selected to generate a stylized cartoon character.

What we do with your child's photo

The photo never leaves your device. In the current version of the app, the photo is read directly in your browser or app and is used only to show a local preview. It is not uploaded to our servers, not sent to any AI image service, and not stored by us in any form. The character artwork shown in stories is a generic illustrated character, not a rendering of the uploaded photo. If a future version adds AI-based portrait generation from photos, this policy will be updated first and the feature will be off by default until it is.

What we do send to generate a story

The child's first name, age band, favorite color, and chosen story theme are sent to our backend server, which forwards them to Anthropic's Claude API solely to generate the text of the story. No photo, address, school, or other identifying information is included in this request. Anthropic processes this text to return a generated story and does not receive the photo.

Where data is stored, and for how long

Story text you generate is kept only in your browser's active session (and, for signed-in native app use, on your device) so you can revisit past stories. We do not currently operate a server-side database of user profiles, photos, or generated stories. If you close the app/browser and clear its data, your local story history is gone. Purchase and subscription status is managed by our payments provider (RevenueCat) and the app store you purchased through (Apple App Store or Google Play), not by us directly.

Payments

Subscriptions and one-time purchases (such as the printable storybook add-on) are handled entirely by Apple's or Google's in-app purchase systems and by RevenueCat, which manages entitlement status. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR)

Stardust Tales is designed to be used by a parent or guardian, together with or on behalf of their child — it is not intended for a child to download or use unsupervised. We do not knowingly collect data directly from children without a parent or guardian initiating and controlling the session. Because the app does not transmit the child's photo anywhere, and only transmits a first name, an age band, and a color/theme preference for the sole purpose of generating that session's story, we believe the amount of personal data leaving the device is minimal. That said, a first name and age combined could be considered personal data under GDPR and COPPA, and we recommend using an initial, nickname, or short story-character name rather than a full legal name when personalizing stories.

Third-party services we use

Anthropic (Claude API) — generates story text server-side, from the limited fields described above, not including any photo.
RevenueCat — manages subscription/purchase entitlement status.
Apple App Store / Google Play — process payments and distribute the app.

We do not use third-party advertising or behavioral-advertising SDKs in this app.

Your rights / requesting deletion

Because story data lives in your local app/browser session rather than in a server-side account tied to you, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. If you believe any data was nonetheless retained on our servers and you would like it deleted, or you have any other privacy question, contact us using the details below and we will investigate and respond.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change what data we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
