Who can use this app

Stardust Tales is intended to be used by an adult parent or legal guardian, on behalf of their child, to generate personalized bedtime stories. By using the app you confirm that you are the parent or legal guardian of any child whose name, age, or photo you enter, or that you have that guardian's permission to do so.

Subscriptions and purchases

Some features (additional stories per week/month, the printable storybook) require a paid subscription or one-time purchase, billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play using the price shown at the time of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled through your app store account; cancel any time before renewal to avoid the next charge. One-time purchases (such as the storybook add-on) are not subscriptions and are not automatically renewed.

Content generated by the app

Stories are generated automatically by an AI model based on the details you provide. We do not review each story before it is shown to you. If a generated story contains anything inappropriate, please stop using it and contact us using the details below so we can investigate.

Acceptable use

Please don't use the app to generate content that is unlawful, harassing, or intended to harm someone, and don't attempt to use the character/story generation features on behalf of a real child without that child's parent or guardian's consent.
